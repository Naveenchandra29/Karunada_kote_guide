# Karunada-Kote Guide – Smart Heritage Tourism & Fort Exploration Platform

## Overview
Karunada-Kote Guide is an Android-based heritage tourism application developed to provide interactive historical exploration of Karnataka’s famous forts and cultural landmarks. The platform enhances visitor engagement through guided storytelling, location-aware navigation, and multilingual historical content.

## Features
- Interactive fort exploration guide
- Historical audio narration
- Location-aware landmark tracking
- Guided map-based navigation
- Heritage storytelling system
- Tourist challenge and activity modules
- Kannada and English language support

## Technologies Used
- Android Studio
- Java/Kotlin
- Google Maps Integration
- MediaPlayer API
- GPS & Location Services
- Image Overlay Concepts

## Problem Statement
Many visitors explore historical forts without understanding their cultural significance, engineering achievements, or historical stories due to lack of accessible and engaging digital guidance.

## App Workflow
- Select and explore a historical fort
- Navigate through important landmarks
- Listen to historical narrations
- Discover engineering and cultural facts
- Participate in heritage-based activities and challenges

## Technical Implementation
- GPS-enabled location tracking for guided exploration
- MediaPlayer integration for audio narration
- Custom map overlays and heritage markers
- Interactive tourist navigation interface

## Impact Goals
- Promote heritage tourism awareness
- Increase cultural and historical engagement
- Support educational tourism experiences
- Preserve and digitally showcase Karnataka’s heritage
